LADS Launch Schedule

The group-specific launch plan and objectives for both days.

DAY 1 — NOVEMBER 4

Launch Night | 5:00 PM – Late

5:00 PM — Pre-Launch

  • Everyone is online and ready at the character selection screen.
  • Final preparations before launch.

6:00 PM — LAUNCH

  • Begin leveling immediately.
  • Prioritize obtaining 6-slot bags from mob drops or have Brandon craft them.
    • Refer to the Bags section of the guide for details.
  • Push to level 13 minimum to qualify for Hall of Thanes.
  • Hall of Thanes takes priority: If you're leveling elsewhere when the group is ready to run it, drop what you're doing and join.

Late Night — Wrap-Up

  • Brandon will log off by 3:00 AM at the latest, potentially earlier.
  • Plan the remaining grind around the group's progress and everyone's availability.

Day 1 Objectives

  • Be online and ready before launch.
  • Secure 6-slot bags.
  • Reach level 13.
  • Complete Hall of Thanes.

DAY 2 — NOVEMBER 5

Westfall Questing | Sleeping Bag Questline | Ruins of Lordaeron | Deadmines

8:00 AM — Early Start

  • Brandon, Anderson, and anyone else available log on and begin questing in Westfall.
  • Prioritize progressing through the Defias Brotherhood questline to prepare for Deadmines.
  • Keep track of where Brandon leaves off so the group can resume together later.

During Brandon's Teaching Hours

  • Continue questing while Brandon is unavailable.
  • Begin and progress through the Sleeping Bag questline.
  • Once that's underway, progress the Defias Brotherhood questline up to the point where Brandon left off.
  • Do not progress beyond Brandon's stopping point. The goal is to reunite and complete the remaining questline together when he returns.

After Brandon Returns — Group Progression

  • Regroup and finish the Defias Brotherhood questline together.
  • Complete the Ruins of Lordaeron corpse run as a group.

Deadmines — When Ready

  • Run Deadmines once the questline is complete and the group is at an appropriate level.
  • If we're underleveled, continue questing until we're ready.

Day 2 Objectives

  • Progress through the Defias Brotherhood questline.
  • Make progress on the Sleeping Bag questline while Brandon is away.
  • Finish the Defias Brotherhood questline together when Brandon returns.
  • Complete the Ruins of Lordaeron corpse run as a group.
  • Run Deadmines if the group's level permits.
