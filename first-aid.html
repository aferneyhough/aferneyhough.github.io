First Aid

Bandage milestones, cloth priorities and potion notes.

  • First Aid now lets you craft potions.
    • Keep any drops of [Peacebloom], [Briarthorn] & [Bruiseweed].
      • These can be combined with [Empty Vial] and [Mild Spices] to make health potions
      • Funnel these to the tank for easy dungeon clears.
  • Keep cloth drops for bandages (and bag upgrades).
  • Key level-ups:
    • [Heavy Linen Bandage] at 40
    • [Minor Healing Potion] at 55
    • [Wool Bandage] at 80
    • [Lesser Healing Potion] at 85
    • [Heavy Wool Bandage] at 115
    • [Healing Potion] at 135
    • [Silk Bandage] at 150
    • [Heavy Silk Bandage] at 180
    • [Mageweave Bandage] at 210
    • [Superior Healing Potion] at 230
    • [Heavy Mageweave Bandage] at 240
    • [Runecloth Bandage] at 260
    • [Heavy Runecloth Bandage] at 290
