Saving for a Mount

The listed mount items are 80g each in the source guide. Confirm the current WoW: Forever vendor price and any reputation discount before purchasing.

  • Prioritize vendor-value loot. Weapons and armor can be sold for significantly more than miscellaneous junk. When bags are full, compare vendor values before deciding what to leave behind.
  • Don't train every ability rank automatically. Skip ranks you genuinely don't use while leveling, but prioritize abilities that improve kill speed or reduce downtime.
  • Vendor efficiently. Plan vendor trips around your quest route instead of making separate trips. Keep bags clear so you don't leave valuable drops behind.
  • Don't overinvest in professions. Training, recipes, and materials can consume your mount savings. Gather while questing and defer expensive profession leveling until after your mount.
  • Check faction reputation discounts. Honored reputation with your racial mount faction can reduce mount-related costs in standard Classic. Verify the discount applies in WoW: Forever before relying on it. On a standard Classic level 40 mount purchase costing 100g total, this saves 10g, reducing the total to 90g.
  • Sell unused green items intelligently. Some are worth more to vendors than they are likely to fetch on the Auction House after fees and delays.
  • Keep your mount fund liquid. Avoid tying up your gold in Auction House speculation or materials you may not sell before level 40. Do not get baited by the Auction House deposit fees.
