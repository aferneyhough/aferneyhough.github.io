Cozy Sleeping Bag Questline

The complete route, coordinates and buff details—nothing else from the guide.

  • The sleeping bag from SoD is back, allowing you up to 3% increased XP when used at a campsite (party members can use it too). When we have considerable downtime (probably when Brandon is teaching) we will get it:

  • Westfall (Start)

    • Take the flight path to Sentinel Hill.

    • Go to Alexston Farmstead (37.4, 50.6).

    • Click on the Burned-Out Remains to pick up your first quest, The Note You Found.

  • The Barrens

    • After Westfall, swim south down the coastline to Booty Bay.
    • You may get aggro’d by higher level mobs in the water. If you die, hope that you get the graveyard next to Booty Bay. Take resurrection sickness and take the boat to Ratchet.

    • Travel to The Barrens, south of Camp Taurajo (46.4, 73.9), to find the next set of burned-out remains.

    • Make your way into Mulgore, north past Thunder Bluff into Stonetalon. We should encounter way less Horde here than in the Barrens.

  • Stonetalon Mountains

    • Take the path northeast of Sun Rock Retreat to an abandoned camp.

    • Click the Pocket Litter (40.8, 52.6), then continue to the Mound of Dirt (39.6, 49.9) which is nearby.

    • Use your Hearthstone and make your way to Ironforge (flight path or tram, depending where your hearth is).
    • Walk through Dun Morogh into Loch Modan. Get the flight path at Thelsammar.

  • Loch Modan

    • Head to the Loch Modan Dam (49.4, 12.9).

    • Carefully jump down to the ledge below and interact with the Eagle Figurine (Carved Figurine).

    • Jump down into the water. Make your way to Menethil Harbor. Get the flight path and take the boat from Menethil Harbor to Southshore, then head to Thoradin’s Wall.

  • Hillsbrad Foothills / Arathi Highlands (Final Reward)

    • Travel to the border near Thoradin's Wall in Arathi Highlands (22.5, 24.2).

    • Follow the wall to the fallen cart and complete the jumping puzzle.

    • Click the Messenger Bag, then the Hastily Rolled-Up Satchel to complete the questline and obtain the Cozy Sleeping Bag.

      • It takes 15 seconds to unfurl the sleeping bag
      • Each stack takes 1 minute to cumulate, taking 3 minutes total to earn the +3% XP buff
      • Despawns after 20 minutes
      • Group members can use your bag once unrolled (a full group of 5 can sleep in a single bag, limit unknown if any)
      • Buff affects quest XP rewards and kill XP
      • Buff persists through death.
