WEAPON PROGRESSION

Weapon milestones are separated strictly by weapon type. Every item kept on this page has a direct WoWhead Forever item page; entries without a confirmed Forever mapping are excluded rather than guessed.

Important: this is a curated progression reference, not a list of every weapon in the game. We are prioritizing meaningful upgrades for THE LADS and keeping the page clean until additional Forever mappings are individually verified.

CLASS NOTES

HOW THE GROUP SHOULD USE THIS PAGE

Warrior: weapon upgrades are major leveling power spikes, so prioritize meaningful two-handed weapon damage upgrades. Enhancement Shaman: use the confirmed two-handed milestones that fit your trained weapon skills. Feral Druid: Forever weapon scaling should be evaluated separately by confirmed item and weapon mechanics, so no Druid-specific weapon recommendation is listed here without a validated Forever source. Rogue and Priest: their weapon-type sections will only be restored after their specific items and WoWhead Forever mappings are individually validated.
