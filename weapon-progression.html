THE ALLIANCE FIELD GUIDE

WEAPON PROGRESSION

Key weapon upgrades for THE LADS from early leveling through the late-game dungeon bracket. This guide is organized by weapon type so the group can quickly see shared upgrades and class-specific milestones.

How to use this guide: levels are approximate acquisition levels. Every highlighted item links directly to its WoWhead Forever page. For Feral Druids, prioritize the highest usable 2H weapon DPS; for Enhancement, prioritize strong two-handers and your best available weapon damage profile.

BUILD NOTES

CLASS PRIORITY QUICK REFERENCE

Warrior: chase meaningful weapon damage upgrades; 2H milestones are the main route.   Enhancement Shaman: prioritize strong two-handers.   Feral Druid: follow the highest usable 2H weapon DPS.   Rogue: use the Sword or Dagger path that matches your build and keep both hands current.   Priest: keep your wand upgraded and choose caster weapons based on your spell/healing build.
