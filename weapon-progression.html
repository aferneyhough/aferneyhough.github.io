WEAPON PROGRESSION

A deliberately curated list of weapon milestones with links that have been confirmed against WoWhead's Forever database. Rather than keep questionable IDs on the page, unconfirmed mappings have been removed.

How to use this guide: acquisition levels are approximate. This page is organized by weapon type so shared upgrades are easy to reference. More weapons can be added only once their exact WoWhead Forever item mapping is verified.

CLASS PRIORITY QUICK REFERENCE

Warrior: weapon upgrades are among your biggest leveling power spikes; prioritize meaningful two-handed weapon damage upgrades.   Enhancement Shaman: prioritize strong two-handed weapons and weapon damage.   Feral Druid: weapon DPS matters in WoW: Forever, so keep your two-hander current.   Rogue & Priest: their dedicated progression tables were removed from this revision because the existing page contained item IDs that were not confidently validated against WoWhead Forever.
