Recommended Addons

The addon recommendations from the original guide.

  • DungeonJournal: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/dungeonjournal
    • Contains dungeon maps, quests, and drops.
  • LeatrixPlus: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/leatrix-plus
    • This add-on is VERY useful. You can automate quest pick-up and turn in and save yourself tons of clicks, among a bunch of useful shit.
  • OpenMap: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/openmap
    • Better map that shows the world map + geographic terrain like caves, etc.
  • ForeverFaces: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/forever-faces
    • 3D animated character portraits - optional
  • Experience Plus: https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/experience-plus/gallery
    • Shows completed quest XP on your bar
