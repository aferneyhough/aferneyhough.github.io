Cooking & XP Food

Food buffs, cooking materials, recipes and skill progression.

  • Keep the following cooking reagents for food buffs and 5% XP gain:
    • [Chunk of Boar Meat] = [Roasted Boar Meat] (strength, XP)
    • [Stringy Wolf Meat] + [Mild Spices] (from vendor) = [Spiced Wolf Meat] (agility, XP)
    • [Small Spider Leg] = [Kaldorei Spider Kabob] (stamina, XP)
      • Obtain the recipe through the Recipe of the Kaldorei quest in Dolanaar, Teldrassil.
      • This is an Alliance-friendly alternative to Crispy Bat Wing.
    • [Small Egg] + [Mild Spices] (from vendor) = [Herb Baked Egg] (stamina, XP)
      • Keep Small Eggs for other recipes as well.
    • [Raw Brilliant Smallfish] = [Brilliant Smallfish] (XP food)
      • Buy the recipe from Catherine Leland in Stormwind.
      • These are useful for leveling Cooking from 1–50.
    • [Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper] = [Longjaw Mud Snapper] (attack power, XP)
      • Buy the recipe from Tharynn Bouden in Goldshire.
      • Keep Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper whenever possible to maintain a constant supply of XP food for the group.
      • Buy these from vendors or the Auction House when convenient.
    • [Raw Bristle Whisker Catfish] = [Bristle Whisker Catfish] (XP food)
      • Buy the recipe from Catherine Leland in Stormwind.
      • Keep these for leveling Cooking from 100–150.
    • [Raw Mithril Head Trout] = [Mithril Head Trout] (XP food)
      • Keep these for later Cooking progression.
      • The recipe requires 175 Cooking, so this is not an immediate priority.
    • [Stringy Vulture Meat] + [Murloc Eye] + [Goretusk Snout] = [Westfall Stew]
      • Keep these ingredients while questing in Westfall.
      • The recipe is a useful pickup while completing quests in the zone.
    • [Small Venom Sac] + [Mild Spices] + [Refreshing Spring Water] = [Venomous Smoothie]
      • The recipe is sold by Kalsey Sanden in Redridge Mountains.
      • Requires 25 Cooking.
    • [Small Egg] + [Simple Flour] = [Skywall Souffle]
      • Obtain the recipe through the Restocking the Larders quest.
      • Requires 25 Cooking.
    • [Small Egg] + [Mild Spices] = [Breakfast Omelette]
      • The recipe drops from Dire Condors.
      • Requires 75 Cooking.
    • [Small Egg] = [Peace Tea] ingredients check
      • Keep Small Eggs, but verify the exact ingredients before farming specifically for this recipe.
      • The recipe drops from Frostmane Seers in Dun Morogh.
      • Requires 25 Cooking.
  • Keep the following additional cooking materials while leveling:
    • [Chunk of Boar Meat]
      • Used for Roasted Boar Meat.
      • Commonly collected from boars in early leveling zones.
    • [Stringy Wolf Meat]
      • Used for Spiced Wolf Meat.
      • Keep these while killing wolves in early zones.
    • [Small Spider Leg]
      • Used for Kaldorei Spider Kabob.
      • Keep these while questing around Teldrassil.
    • [Small Egg]
      • Used for several low-level and mid-level recipes.
      • Keep these instead of vendoring them.
    • [Stringy Vulture Meat]
      • Used for Westfall Stew.
      • Keep while questing in Westfall.
    • [Murloc Eye]
      • Used for Westfall Stew.
      • Keep these while killing murlocs in Westfall.
    • [Goretusk Snout]
      • Used for Westfall Stew.
      • Keep these while killing goretusks in Westfall.
    • [Small Venom Sac]
      • Used for Venomous Smoothie.
      • Keep these if you collect them while questing.
    • [Raw Brilliant Smallfish]
      • Used for Brilliant Smallfish.
      • Keep these if you are fishing or if you buy them cheaply.
    • [Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper]
      • Used for Longjaw Mud Snapper.
      • Keep these for the group XP food supply.
    • [Raw Bristle Whisker Catfish]
      • Used for Bristle Whisker Catfish.
      • Keep these for Cooking progression.
    • [Raw Mithril Head Trout]
      • Used for Mithril Head Trout.
      • Keep these for later cooking progression.
    • [Bear Meat]
      • Keep while leveling if you have spare bag space.
      • Check recipes before vendoring.
    • [Crawler Meat]
      • Keep while leveling if you have spare bag space.
      • Check recipes before vendoring.
    • [Crawler Claw]
      • Keep while leveling if you have spare bag space.
      • Check recipes before vendoring.
    • [Lean Wolf Flank]
      • Keep if collected, but do not farm specifically for it before checking the recipes available to you.
  • Buy the following ingredients from Cooking Supply vendors when needed:
    • [Mild Spices]
    • [Simple Flour]
    • [Refreshing Spring Water]
    • Other spices required by recipes you have learned.
    • Do not waste bag space carrying excessive quantities of vendor ingredients. Buy them when you are ready to cook.
  • Cooking leveling materials:
    • Cooking 1–50: [Raw Brilliant Smallfish] (approximately 52)
    • Cooking 50–100: [Raw Longjaw Mud Snapper] (approximately 55)
    • Cooking 100–150: [Raw Bristle Whisker Catfish] (approximately 55)
    • Cooking 150–175: [Raw Bristle Whisker Catfish] (approximately 65)
    • Cooking 175–225: [Raw Mithril Head Trout] (approximately 55)
      • You will need the Expert Cookbook to progress beyond 150 Cooking.
      • Alliance players can buy it from Shandrina in Ashenvale.
      • These quantities are estimates. Keep extra materials because Cooking skill-ups are not guaranteed on every craft.

  • Cooking supplies and campfires:
    • Cooking requires a nearby fire.
    • Use existing fires in inns and towns whenever possible.
    • Keep an eye out for the Basic Campfire recipe from your Cooking trainer.
    • Cooking can also be useful for preparing food for the group during dungeon breaks and grinding sessions.
    • Later, additional campfire recipes can increase the capacity of camp buffs, so keep an eye out for these as cooking progresses.
  • General rule: Keep all cooking reagents.
    • Keep cooking materials while questing instead of farming them separately whenever possible.
    • Prioritize materials that contribute to XP food or Cooking progression.
    • If bags become full, vendor excess materials that are easily replaced before getting rid of uncommon or zone-specific reagents.
    • Cook during downtime, especially while travelling or waiting for dungeon groups.
    • Eat for at least 10 seconds to receive the Well Fed buff and its 5% XP bonus from kills.
    • The Well Fed buff lasts 15 minutes, so refresh it regularly while grinding.
    • Keep a supply of cooked food available for every group member.
    • Prioritize maintaining the XP buff over chasing minor stat upgrades.
