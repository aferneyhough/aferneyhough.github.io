Bags & Inventory

Bag upgrades, tailoring materials and the group bag plan.

  • Tailoring can craft 6 slot bags for 6 [Linen Cloth] and 3 [Coarse Thread] per.
    • Total linen needed: 24
    • Total [coarse thread] needed: 12
  • Tailoring can also craft a 6 slot reagent bag for your professions, with 12 [Linen Cloth] and 6 [Coarse Thread] per.
  • If you do not have full 6 slot bags BEFORE we run HoT, this needs to be a priority.
  • For Brandon: pick up tailoring early from Trianna in Craftsmen’s Terrace in Darnassus.
  • Getting bags early may be a little convoluted because Brandon will be starting in Teldrassil (Night Elf), so here is how to get around it:
    • Create a level 1 Night Elf and run to Dolanaar. Log off and log back on your main.
    • Mail the mats for Linen Bags to your alt, log off, get back on your alt. Mail is instant between same-account characters.
    • Trade mats to Brandon for him to craft and then mail the bags back to your main.
    • Login to your main and get bags. ez.
