Pre-Leveling

Everything to handle before launch: login timing, bank characters, efficient early-game habits, and quest materials worth saving.

  • Pre-Leveling (All):
    • Be online well before 6pm. In the 2019 Classic, servers opened an hour before launch. You were able to login and bypass the login server queue entirely, and then login to your char exactly at 6pm.
      • Do not get past the queue then say “aight ima go workout”.
    • Create a bank character that you can send profession mats to while you level to conserve bag space. Your mail is valid for 30 days. You can do this during the name reservation period if you bought it.
    • Always be killing things that give you XP. If you are moving between quest objectives, grind boars etc. on the way to both get cooking reagents and XP.
      • Configure your swing timer if you want to make this easier so you can see when to turn to the mob to swing, then keep running, repeat.
      • Running at an angle will prevent you from being dazed, if you get dazed it's a legit skill issue.
    • Connect an authenticator to your Battle.net account for +4 bag slots.
    • Call out crafting mats and see if anyone needs them. The economy is fresh so they will not sell for much anyway - it is better to use internally in the group for profession and crafted gear.
      • Spiders Silk, Lustrous Pearl, spare ore, spare linen, etc.
    • Basic Campfire Kits can be created with 1 [Flint and Tinder] and 1 [Simple Wood].
      • You only need 1 [Flint and Tinder], but keep a stack or so of [Simple Wood].
    • These are ordinary mob drops needed for Alliance quests between levels 1 and 30. They are not classified as Quest Items and can be collected before accepting their associated quests. Save the listed quantities while leveling to avoid having to farm these materials later. Items are organized by the zone where they are most useful.
