Hall of Thanes

Recommended dungeon level: 13–18
Location: Beneath Ironforge

  • Important Heirlooms
    • Minimum Level: 10
    • Quest Giver: Thom Filch
    • Location: Old Ironforge (32.6, 44.6)
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Available outside the dungeon.
  • The Restless Dead
    • Minimum Level: 10
    • Quest Giver: Afadra Dunwall
    • Location: Ironforge
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Verify the NPC's precise location in-game, as the listed coordinates may be inconsistent.
  • Old Ironforge Incursion
    • Minimum Level: 9
    • Quest Giver: Earthseer Farsen
    • Location: Gol'Golar Quarry, Dun Morogh
    • Notes: Requires the Underground Map, obtained through the Dark Iron Map item.
  • The Treaty of Understanding
    • Minimum Level: 9
    • Quest Giver: Interactable quest item
    • Location: Inside Hall of Thanes, in the Reliquary of Kings
    • Notes: Pick up the quest inside the dungeon.
  • An Ancient Grudge
    • Minimum Level: 10
    • Quest Giver: Ghostly Attendant
    • Location: Anvilmar's Rest, inside Hall of Thanes
    • Notes: Quest is obtained inside the dungeon.

Ruins of Lordaeron

Recommended dungeon level: 16–23
Location: Above Undercity, Tirisfal Glades

  • Abominable Creatures
    • Minimum Level: 16
    • Quest Giver: Captain Truman
    • Location: Inside Ruins of Lordaeron, at the dungeon entrance
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Captain Truman is Alliance-friendly and Horde-hostile. Return the Head of the Baron to Captain Truman.
  • Bloodied Insignia
    • Minimum Level: 16
    • Quest Giver: General Marcus Jonathan
    • Location: Stormwind City (turn-in); quest starts from undead mobs inside Ruins of Lordaeron
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Collect 10 Bloodied Insignias from undead mobs and take them to General Marcus Jonathan.
  • [Crest of Lordaeron]
    • Minimum Level: 16
    • Quest Giver: Interactable quest object
    • Location: Inside Ruins of Lordaeron; the Crest can spawn in multiple locations
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Find the Crest of Lordaeron and return it to Stormwind City. Wowhead notes multiple possible spawn points.
  • Remember That I Love You
    • Minimum Level: 15
    • Quest Giver: [Blood-Stained Letter]
    • Location: Inside Ruins of Lordaeron, near Rath'mael
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Bring the Blood-Stained Letter to Orphan Matron Nightingale in Stormwind City.

The Deadmines

Recommended dungeon level: 14–24
Location: Moonbrook, Westfall

  • Collecting Memories
    • Minimum Level: 14
    • Quest Giver: Wilder Thistlenettle
    • Location: Stormwind, Dwarven District
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Can be picked up in Stormwind.
  • Oh Brother...
    • Minimum Level: 15
    • Quest Giver: Wilder Thistlenettle
    • Location: Stormwind, Dwarven District
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Can be picked up alongside Collecting Memories.
  • Underground Assault
    • Minimum Level: 15
    • Quest Giver: Shoni the Shilent
    • Location: Stormwind, Dwarven District
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Collect the required items while clearing the dungeon.
  • Red Silk Bandanas
    • Minimum Level: 14
    • Quest Giver: Scout Riell
    • Location: Westfall, Sentinel Hill
    • Notes: Requires completing six quests in the Defias Brotherhood quest chain. Start with The Defias Brotherhood from Gryan Stoutmantle.
  • The Defias Brotherhood
    • Minimum Level: 14
    • Quest Giver: Gryan Stoutmantle
    • Location: Westfall, Sentinel Hill
    • Notes: Alliance quest chain. Complete the prerequisite quests before finishing the chain.
  • Destruction in Deadmines
    • Minimum Level: 9
    • Quest Giver: Quest chain beginning with Testing the Wells from Alba Fairmoon
    • Location: Alba Fairmoon, Westfall
    • Notes: Part of a quest chain. Complete the prerequisites before entering the dungeon.
  • The Unsent Letter
    • Minimum Level: 16
    • Quest Giver: Edwin VanCleef (quest item drop)
    • Location: Inside The Deadmines
    • Notes: Loot the letter from Edwin VanCleef. This quest is also a prerequisite for The Stockade Riots.
  • The Test of Righteousness
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Jordan Stilwell
    • Location: Ironforge
    • Notes: Paladin-only. Requires the Tome of Valor quest chain.

Blackfathom Deeps

Recommended dungeon level: 24–32
Location: Northwest Ashenvale, near Zoram Strand

  • Knowledge in the Deeps
    • Minimum Level: 18
    • Quest Giver: Gerrig Bonegrip
    • Location: Ironforge, Forlorn Cave
    • Notes: Alliance quest. One of the earliest available BFD quests.
  • Researching the Corruption
    • Minimum Level: 18
    • Quest Giver: Gershala Nightwhisper
    • Location: Darkshore, Auberdine
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before travelling to Ashenvale.
  • In Search of Thaelrid
    • Minimum Level: 18
    • Quest Giver: Dawnwatcher Shaedlass
    • Location: Darnassus, Craftsman's Terrace
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Required before obtaining Blackfathom Villainy.
  • Blackfathom Villainy
    • Minimum Level: 18
    • Quest Giver: Argent Guard Thaelrid
    • Location: Inside Blackfathom Deeps, alcove southwest of Ghamoo-ra
    • Notes: Requires completing In Search of Thaelrid first.
  • Twilight Falls
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Argent Guard Manados
    • Location: Darnassus, Craftsman's Terrace
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.

The Stockades

Recommended dungeon level: 22–30
Location: Stormwind City

  • The Stockade Riots
    • Minimum Level: 16
    • Quest Giver: Warden Thelwater
    • Location: Outside The Stockades, Stormwind
    • Notes: Requires a quest chain beginning with The Unsent Letter from The Deadmines.
  • Quell the Uprising
    • Minimum Level: 22
    • Quest Giver: Warden Thelwater
    • Location: Outside The Stockades, Stormwind
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • The Color of Blood
    • Minimum Level: 22
    • Quest Giver: Nikova Raskol
    • Location: Stormwind, Old Town
    • Notes: NPC patrols the area. Locate them before entering the dungeon.
  • Crime and Punishment
    • Minimum Level: 22
    • Quest Giver: Councilman Millstipe
    • Location: Duskwood, Darkshire
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before travelling to Stormwind.
  • What Comes Around...
    • Minimum Level: 22
    • Quest Giver: Guard Berton
    • Location: Redridge Mountains, Lakeshire
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • The Fury Runs Deep
    • Minimum Level: 25
    • Quest Giver: Motley Garmason
    • Location: Wetlands, Dun Modr
    • Notes: Requires completing The Dark Iron War first.

Excavation Site: Wetlands

Location: Whelgar's Excavation Site, Wetlands

  • Highland Hides
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: James Halloran
    • Location: Menethil Harbor, Wetlands
    • Notes: Requires completing Daily Delivery first.
  • Songblade Search
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Dorin Songblade
    • Location: Redridge Mountains
    • Notes: Pick it up before travelling to the dungeon.
  • Horrors in the Highland
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Rethiel the Greenwarden
    • Location: Wetlands
    • Notes: Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • Lost in the Thicket Things
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Llana
    • Location: Ashenvale
    • Notes: Starts with Seeking Caitlin. Complete the required quest chain.
  • Heartwoven
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Follow-up quest from Lost in the Thicket Things
    • Location: Follow the quest chain from Ashenvale
    • Notes: Requires completing Lost in the Thicket Things first.
  • Lost Relic Carry
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: [Titan Relic] (quest-starting item)
    • Location: Inside Excavation Site: Wetlands
    • Notes: Loot the Titan Relic to begin the quest.
  • Prehistoric Prism
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Follow-up quest from Lost Relic Carry
    • Location: Follow the quest chain from Excavation Site
    • Notes: Requires completing Lost Relic Carry first.
  • Changing Tastes
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Not confirmed in the current guide
    • Location: Not confirmed
    • Notes: Quest-giver details remain unverified in the beta guide.
  • Elder Knowledge
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: [Titan Relic] (quest-starting item)
    • Location: Inside Excavation Site: Wetlands
    • Notes: Loot the Titan Relic to begin the quest.
  • Earthen Echo
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Follow-up quest from Elder Knowledge
    • Location: Follow the quest chain from Excavation Site
    • Notes: Requires completing Elder Knowledge first.

City of Dalaran

Gnomeregan

Recommended dungeon level: 29–38
Location: Gnomeregan, Dun Morogh

  • Save Techbot's Brain!
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Tinkmaster Overspark
    • Location: Ironforge, Tinkertown
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • Gyrodrillmatic Excavationators
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Shoni the Shilent
    • Location: Stormwind, Dwarven Quarter
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before travelling to Gnomeregan.
  • Gnogaine
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Ozzie Togglevolt
    • Location: Dun Morogh, Kharanos
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • The Only Cure is More Green Glow
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Ozzie Togglevolt
    • Location: Dun Morogh, Kharanos
    • Notes: Requires completing Gnogaine first.
  • A Fine Mess
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Kernobee (escort NPC)
    • Location: Inside Gnomeregan, in a room to the right of the Clean Room
    • Notes: Escort quest. Be prepared to protect Kernobee throughout the escort.
  • Essential Artificials
    • Minimum Level: 24
    • Quest Giver: Klockmort Spannerspan
    • Location: Ironforge, Tinkertown
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • Data Rescue
    • Minimum Level: 25
    • Quest Giver: Master Mechanic Castpipe
    • Location: Ironforge, Tinkertown
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • The Grand Betrayal
    • Minimum Level: 25
    • Quest Giver: High Tinker Mekkatorque
    • Location: Ironforge, Tinkertown
    • Notes: Alliance quest. Pick it up before entering the dungeon.
  • The Sparklematic 5200!
    • Minimum Level: 25
    • Quest Giver: Sparklematic 5200
    • Location: Inside Gnomeregan
    • Notes: Requires a Grime-Encrusted Object. Use the machine to complete the quest.
  • Grime-Encrusted Ring
    • Minimum Level: 28
    • Quest Giver: [Grime-Encrusted Ring] (item drop)
    • Location: Inside Gnomeregan
    • Notes: Starts the Return of the Ring quest chain.

Access note: At least one party member needs a Workshop Key to open the back door. Lockpicking skill 150 can also open it.

Razorfen Kraul

Recommended dungeon level: 30–40
Location: Southern Barrens

  • Mortality Wanes
    • Minimum Level: 25
    • Quest Giver: Heralath Fallowbrook
    • Location: Inside Razorfen Kraul, behind the main boss
    • Notes: Alliance quest. The giver is inside the dungeon.
  • The Crone of the Kraul
    • Minimum Level: 29
    • Quest Giver: Falfindel Waywarder
    • Location: Feralas, The Lower Wilds
    • Notes: Requires completing Lonebrow's Journal first.
  • Blueleaf Tubers
    • Minimum Level: 20
    • Quest Giver: Mebok Mizzyrix
    • Location: The Barrens, Ratchet
    • Notes: Collect the required quest items before leaving the dungeon.
  • Willix the Importer
    • Minimum Level: 22
    • Quest Giver: Willix the Importer
    • Location: Inside Razorfen Kraul, in a tent near the final boss
    • Notes: Escort quest. Be prepared to protect Willix during the escort.
