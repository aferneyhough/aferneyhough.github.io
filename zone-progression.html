LADS FOREVER • ALLIANCE FIELD GUIDE

Zone Progression

A practical, level-ordered route through Azeroth. Each zone includes its recommended level range and the quest chains, rewards, hubs, and dungeon links worth knowing.

Starting Zones

Durotar

LEVEL 1–10

Horde starting zone. Follow the early Valley of Trials and Sen’jin Village chains; useful as a reference for Horde-only routes, not an Alliance detour.

Mulgore

LEVEL 1–10

Horde starting zone built around Bloodhoof Village and Thunder Bluff. Keep this as a Horde route reference rather than part of the Alliance path.

Teldrassil

LEVEL 1–10

Night Elf starting zone. Complete the Shadowglen and Dolanaar quest clusters before moving toward Darnassus and the boat to Darkshore.

Elwynn Forest

LEVEL 1–10

Human starting zone. Clear Northshire, Goldshire, and the surrounding farms; this is the natural launch point for the Westfall and Deadmines quest chains.

Dun Morogh

LEVEL 1–10

Dwarf and Gnome starting zone. Work through Coldridge Valley and Kharanos, then use Ironforge as the hub for early profession and dungeon quests.

Tirisfal Glades

LEVEL 1–10

Undead starting zone. Brill is the main early hub; included for completeness, but it is not part of the Alliance leveling route.

Zephras Isle

LEVEL 1–12

New Skyborne starting zone. Its opening storyline carries characters through the early levels before they choose their faction; see the Forever zone overview for the new-zone details.

Early Leveling · 10–20

The Barrens

LEVEL 10–20

Large cross-faction leveling zone with long travel distances. Ratchet is a useful neutral hub; it also anchors the Sleeping Bag quest chain and several early dungeon quests.

Silverpine Forest

LEVEL 10–20

Horde leveling zone centered on the Sepulcher. Keep it as a reference for cross-faction dungeon prerequisites and the Ruins of Lordaeron story.

Westfall

LEVEL 10–20

Core Alliance follow-up to Elwynn. Start the Defias Brotherhood chain at Sentinel Hill early; it leads into The Deadmines and provides a dense run of quest XP.

Darkshore

LEVEL 10–20

Major Night Elf questing zone with Auberdine as its hub. Strong follow-up to Teldrassil, with a boat connection and early Blackfathom Deeps quest starts.

Loch Modan

LEVEL 10–20

Alliance-friendly zone with Thelsamar as the hub. Good follow-up to Dun Morogh; the Ironband’s Excavation Site chain also feeds into later Uldaman progression.

Redridge Mountains

LEVEL 15–25

Lakeshire is the main hub. Continue Alliance questing here after Westfall or Loch Modan; several chains lead toward dungeon objectives and later progression.

Mid-Leveling · 15–30

Stonetalon Mountains

LEVEL 15–25

Contested zone with long travel routes and useful mid-teen quest clusters. A natural bridge from The Barrens toward Ashenvale and Desolace.

Duskwood

LEVEL 18–30

Darkshire is the Alliance hub. Follow the local story chains for steady XP and keep an eye on quests that connect to the Stockades and later dungeon content.

Ashenvale

LEVEL 19–30

Important Alliance zone with Astranaar as a major hub. The Raene’s Cleansing chain can award Dartol’s Rod of Transformation , a reusable toy that turns you into a furbolg. Stop before turning in the final quest if you want to keep the original rod.

Hillsbrad Foothills

LEVEL 20–30

Contested zone with Southshore as the Alliance hub. Useful for the mid-20s, but expect PvP traffic and keep travel routes in mind.

Wetlands

LEVEL 20–30

Menethil Harbor is the Alliance travel hub. Pick up the local quest chains and the Excavation Site: Wetlands dungeon quests; the zone also connects several longer Alliance routes.

Thousand Needles

LEVEL 25–35

Shimmering Flats provides a concentrated quest hub with racing, goblin, and centaur-related objectives. A useful transition zone before the mid-30s.

Midgame · 25–45

Alterac Mountains

LEVEL 30–40

A contested highland zone with scattered quest hubs. It also contains the new City of Dalaran dungeon, making it relevant for group progression.

Arathi Highlands

LEVEL 30–40

Refuge Pointe is the Alliance hub. Strong mid-level questing and a major travel corridor between Hillsbrad, Wetlands, and the higher-level Eastern Kingdoms.

Desolace

LEVEL 30–40

A wide zone with multiple faction camps and centaur quest lines. Shen’Dralas adds new story context nearby; plan travel carefully because hubs are spread out.

Stranglethorn Vale

LEVEL 30–45

Booty Bay is the central neutral hub. Expect contested-zone PvP; the Gurubashi Arena and long quest chains make this a high-value but travel-heavy zone.

Badlands

LEVEL 35–45

Kargath and the surrounding camps anchor the zone. Complete the Lotwil quest chain—ending with This Is Going to Be Hard —to earn Nifty Stopwatch , a strong PvP utility trinket with a short movement-speed burst.

Dustwallow Marsh

LEVEL 35–45

Theramore Isle is the Alliance hub. Dense questing around the marsh and the Stonemaul area makes this a useful mid-to-late 30s destination; it also starts some class-specific Uldaman follow-ups.

Swamp of Sorrows

LEVEL 35–45

Stonard and the Swamp’s scattered camps support the late-30s bracket. A smaller quest pool makes it a useful supplement rather than a full leveling destination.

Riverglades

LEVEL 35–45

New Forever frontier zone focused on trade routes, settlements, and local conflicts. Reach it via the Steamwheedle boat route to Powderfuse Port; expect a substantial mid-30s-to-mid-40s questing arc.

Shen'dralas

LEVEL TBD

New zone between Mulgore and Desolace, centered on the Shen’dralar and the centaur conflicts of the region. Its quest lines connect into Dire Maul, Razorfen Downs, and the wider Feralas/Desolace story.

Late Leveling · 40–55

Feralas

LEVEL 40–50

Feathermoon Stronghold is the Alliance hub. Long quest chains and access to Dire Maul make this a key zone as you move into the 40s.

Tanaris

LEVEL 40–50

Gadgetzan is the central hub. The zone offers dense desert questing, Zul’Farrak progression, and several useful travel links for the later game.

The Hinterlands

LEVEL 40–50

Aerie Peak is the Alliance hub. Complete the local Wildhammer and troll-related quest clusters; the zone is a strong source of XP and reputation before the 50s.

Searing Gorge

LEVEL 43–50

Thorium Point is the main hub. A useful late-40s zone with long chains tied to the Dark Iron dwarves and Blackrock Mountain progression.

Azshara

LEVEL 45–55

A remote zone with scattered quest hubs and long travel distances. Useful for filling gaps in the 40s and 50s; plan around the limited routes in and out.

Felwood

LEVEL 47–54

Emerald Sanctuary is the Alliance hub. Follow the corruption-cleansing chains and gather the local reputation quests as you move toward endgame zones.

Blasted Lands

LEVEL 47–55

Nethergarde Keep is the Alliance foothold. The zone is known for repeatable material turn-ins and a concentrated set of high-level quests.

Un'Goro Crater

LEVEL 48–55

Marshal’s Refuge is the main hub. Collect dinosaur materials and complete the exploration chains; the zone is also a key lead-in to Silithus and late-game progression.

Burning Steppes

LEVEL 50–58

Morgan’s Vigil is the Alliance hub. Questing here connects directly to Blackrock Mountain, Blackrock Depths, and Blackrock Spire preparation.

Endgame · 50–60

Western Plaguelands

LEVEL 51–58

Andorhal and Chillwind Camp anchor the zone. Work through the Plaguewood and Scarlet-related chains as preparation for the Eastern Plaguelands.

Eastern Plaguelands

LEVEL 53–60

Light’s Hope Chapel is the Alliance hub. High-level questing, Argent Dawn reputation, and the final push toward level 60.

Winterspring

LEVEL 53–60

Everlook is the central neutral hub. High-level quests, reputation grinds, and long chains make this a strong alternative to the Plaguelands.

Deadwind Pass

LEVEL 55–60

A sparse, dangerous endgame zone with limited questing. Best treated as a targeted destination rather than a full leveling circuit.

Mount Hyjal

LEVEL 55–60

New Forever endgame zone set in the aftermath of Archimonde’s defeat. Explore Darkwhisper Gorge and its restoration story; this is a late-game destination.

Silithus

LEVEL 55–60

Cenarion Hold is the hub. Finish the high-level quest chains and reputation objectives here as part of the final stretch to 60.
